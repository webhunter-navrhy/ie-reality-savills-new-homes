Launching Autumn 2025

Convent Place is an exclusive development featuring just 23 beautifully designed 3, 4, and 5-bedroom A2-rated homes. Ideally located in the heart of Delgany, County Wicklow, these spacious family homes are built to the highest standards, with exceptional finishes and elegant architectural details throughout.

Residents of Convent Place will enjoy a wealth of nearby amenities, including cafes, restaurants, boutiques, and local shops in both Delgany and the nearby coastal town of Greystones. For leisure and recreation, the area offers easy access to Delgany Golf Club, Greystones Sailing Club, scenic walking trails, and the beautiful Greystones beach.

Perfectly positioned approximately 25 KM from Dublin City Centre, the development is well-served by the DART and Bus services, with excellent road links via the N11 and M50. Families will also benefit from a wide selection of top-quality creches, primary, and secondary schools close by.

Convent Place offers the perfect blend of modern living and village charm—an exceptional opportunity to be part of a vibrant and well-connected community. To learn more about this exciting new development by Orchid Homes, register your interest today at conventplace.ie.
